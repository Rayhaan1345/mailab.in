Why the lab exists

What studies show about learning with chatbots.

A chatbot can answer most school homework in seconds. A student who copies the answer skips the practice that builds the skill. Recent studies measure the cost, and they also show a better way to use the same tools.

17%

Lower scores on a later exam for high school students who practised maths with an unrestricted chatbot, compared with students who never had one. A version that gave hints instead of answers removed most of that loss.

Bastani et al., PNAS, 2025
2×

More than double the learning gains of an in-class active-learning lesson, for students in a Harvard physics course who used an AI tutor built on active learning.

Kestin et al., Scientific Reports, 2025

The same tool can harm learning or help it, depending on how it is used. We teach the helpful way. The student does the reasoning, and the AI asks questions, checks steps and quizzes recall.

Recalling an answer strengthens memory more than rereading it (Roediger and Karpicke, Psychological Science, 2006). For that reason our sessions end with students answering questions.

The method

One question at a time. Try the hint ladder below.

  1. Ask what the student has tried. The tutor starts from the student's own attempt.
  2. Find the one thing that blocks them. Then ask a single question about it.
  3. Raise the help only when the student stalls. A nudge, then the concept, then a worked step, then the full answer.
  4. Fade the hints. As the student gets steps right alone, the tutor says less.
  5. Answer when asked. "Just tell me" gets the full answer, followed by one question on the key step.
  6. End with recall. A short quiz, or a summary in the student's own words.

Problem Solve 2x² − 5x − 3 = 0

  1. TutorWhat have you tried so far?
  2. StudentI know it is a quadratic. I tried to factorise it, but I can't find the factors.

A scripted demonstration of the ladder. With the plugin, Claude follows the same steps on any subject. Facts, definitions and syntax get plain answers, because the questioning is for reasoning.

Socratic Tutor

by Mumbai AI Lab · version 1.0.0

The plugin turns Claude into a tutor that teaches by asking. It finds out what the student has tried, asks the question that leads to the next step and gives the answer once the student has tried or asks for it.

It is instructions only. It runs no code, connects to no server and keeps no record of the student between conversations.

Read the report on how it works, from the Socratic method and the studies behind it to how a Claude plugin loads its skills.
