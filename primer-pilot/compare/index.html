Reader test

Three chapters, three versions

The same chapters written three ways. Tell us which you would rather learn from.

The chapters below exist in versions A, B and C. The facts, the numbers and the interactive figures are the same in all three, and only the way the text is written differs. Sections are arranged in three layers: the idea, how it works, and one closed box called Go deeper that holds the rest. The version called Current is the book as it is today, so that you can compare.

Draft. These versions were published before the last round of fixes. Reviewers found sentences that are wrong or unclear, and the writers are correcting them now. The corrected versions will replace these pages at the same addresses.

What to do

  1. Taste test. For the three chapters, read the first section of versions A, B and C. Then put the three in order, say in one sentence why, and say which one you would keep reading.
  2. Learning test. Your observer will give you three more sections of the chapters, in different versions. Read one, close the page, then answer its two check questions and explain the idea in one sentence. You may answer in English, Hindi or Marathi.
  3. Layout test. On the version you liked best, use the small chip at the bottom right of the page to try the three layouts: Layers, Beside and Steps. Beside works best on a laptop and Steps on a phone.

Please think aloud while you read, and say when a sentence is hard to follow or does not seem to come from the one before.

Questions to answer afterwards

  1. Which version of a chapter would you rather learn from? Why?
  2. Where did you get lost, or want to stop reading?
  3. Did you open Go deeper? What made you open it, or leave it closed?
  4. Did you prefer to see the explanation at once, or one step at a time?
  5. Was it easy to follow the figure and its text together?
  6. Which device did you use, a laptop or a phone?
