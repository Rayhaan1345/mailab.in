How AI Learns

From straight lines to vision-language models

Students at Mumbai AI Lab are building SharkCam, a small underwater camera robot that spots sharks along the Mumbai coast, from the rock pools at Juhu to the fish landings at Sassoon Dock. This primer builds the AI behind it one idea at a time, from a straight line through a scatter of points to models that look at a photo and answer a question about it.

Chapters open with a question to guess before you read, and most figures respond when you drag, slide or press. Sections end with a quick quiz, and chapters end with exercises. Answer in your head before opening an answer; that effort is what makes the ideas stay.
